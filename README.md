# QR-Code-Generator
The QR Code Generator. You can make a QR code for any of your social link/ any text message/ any data  to  show it quickly. Just use it for free and you don't need anyone to pay for a QR.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There are two types of QR in this repository -->
1] QR code, for black and white regular qrcode.
2] Advanced QR code, where you can customize your qrcode according to you 

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q. What you can customize in Advanced QR code option
1] You can customize the dimentions of QR like box_size and font size
2] You can change the color of the QR i.e, a colorful QR
